1 The Farm holding / Desbois Case Study

1.1 Context

The article from Desbois (2008) (available here together with a sample data set) presents a complete case study around the detection of financial risks for farm holdings in France.

Among other methods, Linear Discriminant Analysis and Logistic Regression (plus a stepwise variable selection procedure) are used. ROC curves are provided to compare the methods.

We will reproduce and extend the Desbois case study throughout this course as a running example (“fil rouge”). At each stage, we apply the concepts introduced in the preceding handout.

What you’ll do in this Part 2

Following the concepts from Handout 2: Logistic Regression:

  1. Load and perform EDA on the Desbois dataset. In particular. Understand the 37 financial ratios used as predictors
  2. Reuse the two scores from Part 1 (naive score based on PCA and LDA) as baselines
  3. Evaluate it with a ROC curve
